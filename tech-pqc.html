The quantum threat is real. Quantum computers will eventually break the elliptic curve cryptography that secures today's blockchain systems. We're at the forefront of post-quantum cryptography research and implementation, helping organizations migrate to quantum-safe solutions.

From ML-DSA and Falcon implementations to EIP proposals for Ethereum's post-quantum transition, we're building the cryptographic infrastructure for tomorrow.

Our PQC Implementations

We provide production-grade implementations of NIST-standardized post-quantum algorithms, optimized for blockchain environments:

ML-DSA (Dilithium)

Module-Lattice Digital Signature Algorithm, the primary NIST standard for post-quantum signatures. Our implementations are FIPS 204 compliant and optimized for both embedded systems and smart contract verification.

Falcon

A lattice-based signature scheme offering the smallest signature sizes among PQC candidates. Our Falcon implementations achieve 8x cost improvements in ZK circuits compared to naive approaches.
