Ethereum Improvement Proposals

Contributing to Ethereum's evolution through post-quantum cryptography proposals.

We actively contribute to Ethereum's post-quantum transition through EIP proposals, focusing on bringing quantum-safe signature verification to the protocol level.

EIP-8051 — ML-DSA Signature Verification

Proposes a precompile for ML-DSA (Module-Lattice Digital Signature Algorithm) signature verification. ML-DSA is the primary NIST standard for post-quantum signatures, and this EIP enables efficient on-chain verification of quantum-safe signatures.

EIP-8052 — Falcon Signature Verification

Proposes a precompile for Falcon signature verification. Falcon offers the smallest signature sizes among post-quantum candidates, making it particularly suitable for blockchain applications where storage costs are a concern.

PQBeat

Beyond EIP proposals, we maintain PQBeat, a comprehensive tracking platform for post-quantum readiness across the Ethereum ecosystem. PQBeat evaluates L1/L2 chains, wallets, and protocols using a five-stage framework.
