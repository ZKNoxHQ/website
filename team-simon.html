Simon Masson

Cryptographer

Background

Simon Masson is a cryptologist with 8 years of experience in cryptographic research and development. His career includes roles at Thales and Anoma, where he contributed to cutting-edge work on elliptic curves and zero-knowledge proof systems.

Publications

Simon has authored several academic publications in cryptography, with a focus on elliptic curve design and optimization for cryptographic applications. His research bridges theoretical mathematics with practical implementation challenges.

Bandersnatch Curve

Simon's most notable contribution is co-authoring the Bandersnatch curve specification—a fast embedded curve specifically designed for ECDSA verification circuits. Bandersnatch is optimized for use within zero-knowledge proofs, enabling efficient signature verification in privacy-preserving blockchain applications.

The curve has been adopted by multiple projects in the Ethereum ecosystem and represents a significant advancement in SNARK-friendly cryptographic primitives.

At ZKNOX

At ZKNOX, Simon leads the work on zero-knowledge proof systems and cryptographic curve design. His expertise enables the development of efficient ZK circuits and optimized cryptographic primitives for blockchain applications.
